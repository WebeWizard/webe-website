# Service catalogue and portfolio

**Adopted policy.** The resident declaration, effective date, and exact adopted revision are recorded in `../config.json`. No specific service commitment, priority, delivery authorization, budget, or SLA is approved by this policy alone. Delivery authorization follows the adopted council delegation policy.

**Proposed owner:** Resident Services. **Accountable coordinator:** City Manager. Chief Planner maintains proposed sequencing; the Clerk records resident decisions. These titles do not appoint employees.

## Proposed catalogue

| Service | Eligible input | Proposed owner and output |
|---|---|---|
| Petition and status assistance | A repository-related need or question; identity/authority checked before any reserved decision | Resident Services: minimized case, routing, and honest status |
| Scope and feasibility | A case with outcomes, constraints, and relevant evidence | Chief Planner: options, risk, acceptance criteria, and resource proposal |
| Maintenance, defects, and features | A scoped proposal; delivery also requires a recorded City Council or resident authorization | City Manager coordinates; City Council authorizes or refers; City Engineer delivers authorized orders on a branch; independent Inspector supplies acceptance evidence; resident accepts the pull request |
| Records and access | A sufficiently identified record or correction request | Records Officer: authorized access response, redaction, or linked correction |
| Complaint or appeal | A challenged decision, service failure, or conflict | City Auditor: independent findings for resident resolution; conflicted reviewers must be replaced |
| Incident report | A suspected threat or interruption, without exposing secrets in intake | City Manager: escalation to the responsible human and applicable existing incident process; no emergency authority is created here |

Requests outside this repository's jurisdiction are referred or declined with reasons, not acted on across repositories. An outside contributor can report a need without thereby acquiring resident decision rights.

## Intake, commitments, and capacity

**Inputs:** resident need, applicable authority/policy, affected components, urgency and impact, privacy classification, dependencies, available competence/capacity, and resource estimate.

| Step | Procedure | Resulting record or escalation |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Receive | Confirm the selected repository; minimize personal data; assign a stable supported case identifier. Link duplicates without erasing their history. | Intake record with responsible office, source, timestamp, and unresolved questions |
| 2. Assess | Separate the requested outcome from the proposed solution. Identify risk, reversibility, affected owners, required specialists, dependencies, and authority gaps. | Triage recommendation; incomplete, restricted, or out-of-scope matters go to the Manager/human |
| 3. Prioritize | Compare safety/security impact, existing obligations, defect severity, maintenance debt, resident value, deadline evidence, cost, and capacity. Disclose tradeoffs and conflicts. | Proposed priority and reason; conflicts or changes to reserved priorities require a real resident decision |
| 4. Plan | Maintain one backlog covering defects, maintenance, features, and incidents. Each item needs an owner, state, rationale, dependencies, estimate/uncertainty, and next review. Do not assume unlimited parallel workers. | Visible proposed sequence and deferred obligations, not an authorization |
| 5. Propose a commitment | Specify service owner, eligibility, scope, acceptance criteria, response/update expectations, intended completion window, resource limit, and escalation route. All timing/limits are **TBD by the human**, not template defaults. | Proposed commitment linked to its case; do not label it authorized without actual verified authority |
| 6. Communicate | Report observed progress and blocked dependencies. When a commitment is at risk, notify the resident, propose recovery/reprioritization, and preserve the original baseline. | Dated status/replanning history; no silent deadline reset or backlog disappearance |
| 7. Review outcome | After authorized delivery and inspection, the resident compares actual evidence with the authorized scope and acceptance criteria; require independent inspection and complete records before closure. | Outcome, unresolved obligations, feedback, and any corrective-action links |

Review the portfolio when evidence, capacity, incidents, or resident priorities change and at a human-approved cadence (**TBD**). Reconcile staffing and cost with [resource policy](resources-and-workforce.md); a backlog ranking alone does not commit resources.

## Escalation and records

Capacity overload, incompatible commitments, uncertain jurisdiction, missing expertise, or insufficient authority go to the Manager and actual authorized resident; hold affected actions. A complaint about that handling goes to an unconflicted Auditor or independent human substitute. Silence, inactivity, or an unanswered status message is not consent.

Proposed resulting records are the catalogue revision, case, backlog/commitment history, decision reference, and the authorized work order, inspection, and acceptance decision. These are record requirements, not precreated registers. Apply [records and privacy rules](records-and-privacy.md).
